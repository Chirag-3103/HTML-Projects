Who is a Student??

            A student is defined as a person enrolled in an educational institution,
            such as a school, college, or university, who is actively engaged in learning and acquiring knowledge or skills. 
            The term broadly encompasses K-12 pupils, undergraduates, postgraduates, and even lifelong learners taking courses for personal or professional development.

            Student life is characterized as a transformative phase of self-discovery,
            balancing academic pressures with personal growth, social exploration,
            and the development of critical life skills like time management and resilience.
            While it offers opportunities for joy and friendship, it also involves challenges such as stress,
            financial constraints, and the need to adapt to diverse learning styles and environments.
